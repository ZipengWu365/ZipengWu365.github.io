Research

Temporal structure, similarity, and prediction.

Temporal Representation: A Map of My Research

My published work forms three connected themes: representation and decomposition, similarity and retrieval, and prediction and regression. The map places each paper under its main contribution and connects these themes to my current research.

Common basis: representation theory

My MRes and PhD work studies representation operators Φ: T → R, their composition, and five properties: information preservation, distance preservation, invariance, reconstructability, and commutativity. This framework connects questions about temporal structure with my earlier work on output dependence and interpretable prediction; each task requires different information to be retained.

1. Representation & Decomposition

  • Time-Series Decomposition as a Standalone Task: A Mechanism-Driven Diagnostic Benchmark (ICML 2026). Evaluates component recovery and diagnoses decomposition methods through controlled temporal mechanisms.
  • Non-contact ultrasound testing of batteries (2025 technical report). Uses signal features and acoustic modelling for battery testing.

Software: DeTime and TSDecompose-Benchmark.

2. Similarity & Retrieval

Stationarity-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Time Series Forecasting (SARAF, KDD 2026) selects and aggregates historical segments using temporal similarity, diversity, and stationarity. This connects retrieval directly to forecasting.

Software: EchoTime provides explainable structural comparison of time series and datasets.

3. Prediction & Regression

Output Dependencies & Online Learning

  • A Novel Dynamically Adjusted Regressor Chain for Taxi Demand Prediction (IJCNN 2020). Uses dynamic regressor chains to model dependencies between prediction targets.
  • Correlated Online k-Nearest Neighbors Regressor Chain for Online Multi-output Regression (CONNRC, ICONIP 2023). Learns output dependencies through online kNN regressor chains.
  • A novel online multi-task learning for COVID-19 multi-output spatio-temporal prediction (Heliyon 2023). Combines temporal autocorrelation, spatial dependence, and adaptation to drift.

Interpretable Prediction

  • An Interpretable Multi-target Regression Method for Hierarchical Load Forecasting (ICONIP 2022). Provides interpretable multi-target forecasts of hierarchical electricity demand.
  • iTARGET: Interpretable Tailored Age Regression for Grouped Epigenetic Traits (BIBM 2024). Uses age-specific CpG features and group-specific regression for interpretable age prediction. This is an adjacent application to epigenetic feature vectors.
