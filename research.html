Research

Temporal representations as the common object across time-series ML, foundation models, and world-model rollouts.

Current Research Focuses

  1. Temporal representation for foundation and world models. Temporal abstractions for model rollouts, action histories, state/action sequences, temporal memory, computer-use traces, and VLA trajectories.
  2. Time-series representation learning under non-stationarity. Decomposition, stationarity-aware retrieval, forecasting diagnostics, classification, symbolic regression, and robust evaluation of noisy sequential signals.
  3. Representation-driven research software. Reproducible tools for decomposition and similarity that expose inspectable temporal components, compact JSON outputs, and shareable HTML reports.

Temporal representation

My research centres on temporal representation. The map connects work on time series with broader questions about the representation of sequential data, including action histories and model trajectories.

Shared core: decomposition and structure extraction

Decomposition makes temporal components explicit and inspectable. DeTime provides decomposition tooling; TSDecompose-Benchmark evaluates decomposition as a standalone task, alongside the ICML 2026 paper.

Symbolic representation

Discrete descriptions of temporal structure connect time series to token sequences. Current projects include Time Series Language and Foundation Model and Language-Action Time-Series Tokenization for Efficient VLA Policies. See the associated compute allocations.

Compression

Compact temporal representations that retain information relevant to the task. This direction connects the form of a representation to the information it preserves.

Frequency conversion

A research direction concerned with transforming time-series representations, connected to how temporal information is expressed.

Similarity measurement and retrieval

EchoTime compares time series and datasets through explainable structural similarity. Retrieval also connects comparison to forecasting, as in Stationarity-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Time Series Forecasting (KDD 2026).

Forecasting and multi-output prediction

This thread spans taxi demand prediction, hierarchical load forecasting, spatiotemporal prediction, and retrieval-augmented time-series forecasting. Related work on online multi-output regression connects through learning dependencies between outputs.

Classification · current research

Time-series classification extends the representation question to distinguishing classes from temporal structure.

These directions provide a common lens across time series, spatiotemporal data, action histories, and model trajectories. See the publication list for individual papers, including related regression work.
